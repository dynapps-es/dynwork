## The first Dynwork for Dynapps testers 🚀

Dynwork is Dynapps' desktop app for working with AI agents, built on OpenCode 2.2.5. This first release signs you in to
Dynapps Governance and keeps everything your organization publishes there in sync, so the skills, agents and models
you are allowed to use are ready the moment you open it. It is an internal preview: expect rough edges, and tell us
about them.

- 🔐 **Sign in to Governance:** sign in with your browser, a one-time code or a personal access token. Your token is
  kept in the system's secure storage, and you can stay signed in to several instances and switch between them.
- 🔄 **Always in sync:** the skills, agents, MCP servers and rules your organization publishes arrive on their own, and
  the sync pill at the foot of the sidebar shows how it went. New models and providers show up within 5 minutes, at
  once with Sync now, or when you come back to the window.
- 🧠 **Your organization's models:** model calls go through the Governance gateway with your own access, and the model
  picker lists only the models you may use, each labelled with its region.
- 📱 **Remote control:** pair a phone running Dynwork Mobile by scanning the QR code, or copy the code and paste it
  under Enter the code manually. The connection is end-to-end encrypted.
- 🟢 **Online in Governance:** Governance shows your device online while Dynwork runs.
- 🤖 **Agents, Routines and Brain:** a first look at the views that will bring the Agent Farm and dynbrain into
  Dynwork, under one Agents menu with details in a drawer.
- 🎨 **Dynapps look:** the Dynapps theme by default, midnight in light and pure black in dark, with the Dynwork brand
  in the sidebar and an app icon that follows the system appearance. OpenCode's themes stay in Settings.

### Known limitations

- The installers are not signed by Apple or Microsoft, so the system warns you the first time (see below).
- On macOS Dynwork cannot update itself: download the new DMG from the latest release. On Windows it updates itself.
- This is the first Windows build: tell us anything that does not work there.
