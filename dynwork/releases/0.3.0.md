## Governance's Drive inside Dynwork, and the agent's documents beside each chat 📁

Dynwork now brings in the Drive of your Governance account. You can browse it, read your documents, comment on them
and share them without leaving the app. What the agent writes for you lands in that Drive and opens beside the
conversation that made it.

- 📁 **Drive in the sidebar:** Home, Recent, Mine, Shared with me and From my chats, with search, type filters, list
  or grid, and folders. Each document shows its details, versions, proposals and who can see it. Upload files, or start
  a new document from a Dynapps template.
- 📄 **Documents as they are:**
  - HTML pages look as designed, safely isolated from the app.
  - Markdown can be read at any of its versions.
  - PDFs open in a viewer that follows your theme.
  - Word, Excel and PowerPoint show the Drive's own preview.
- 🧭 **Reading made easy:** an index of the document's headings follows you as you scroll and jumps to any section.
  Both side panels can be resized or hidden. **Present** shows the document alone, full screen (Escape leaves).
- 🤖 **The agent works in the Drive:** what it creates or uploads is filed under `Artifacts/<project>/<conversation>`,
  signed as made by Dynwork, and opens as a tab beside the chat. A counter in the chat's header shows how many
  documents the conversation has.
- 🔒 **You approve every share:** before the agent shares a document, a card above the composer names the person and
  waits for **Allow once**, **Allow in this conversation** or **Decline**.

### Comments on the document 💬

- 🖍️ **See what was commented:** with comments shown, the commented passages are highlighted on the document, and a
  small avatar beside each one shows who wrote it. Clicking a highlight or an avatar opens its thread. Comments sitting
  close together stack, and the open one comes to the front with a ring.
- ✍️ **Comment in place:** with comments shown, select a sentence or click a paragraph, and a box opens right there to
  write your comment. It works in the Drive and beside a chat, on HTML and Markdown documents. The document stays
  exactly where you were reading.
- 🙋 **@Agent in a comment:** mention @Agent and the conversation that last worked with the document wakes up and
  answers in the thread.
- 🔢 **At a glance:** the comments button shows how many threads are open.

### Sharing from Dynwork 🤝

- **Share** opens under its button, with the people who have access and their level. On documents Dynwork's agent made,
  where you are allowed to share, you can invite people, change their level or remove them. For other documents, the
  same panel tells you why and opens the document's sharing page in Governance.

### Also in this release ✨

- ⏳ A document shows a loading animation while it opens, and the chat stays in view meanwhile.
- 🖥️ Dynwork no longer quits a few seconds after launch on some Macs with macOS 26.
- ⏰ A scheduled task's run no longer adds a message to the conversation it belongs to each time it finishes.
- 🟢 The sync status in the sidebar reads clearly over the Dynwork logo.
- 🆕 Brought up to date with the latest OpenCode, including:
  - previous and next buttons in the conversation search;
  - background work shown as running work;
  - several fixes.

### Known limitations

- Comments can be placed on the document itself only in HTML and Markdown. PDFs, Word, Excel and PowerPoint documents
  can be read but not commented on in place.
- Dynwork can change who has access only to documents its agent made. Other documents are shared from Governance.
- The installers are not signed by Apple or Microsoft, so the system warns you the first time (see below).
- macOS and Linux do not update themselves: download the new file from the latest release. Windows updates itself.
